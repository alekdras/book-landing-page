The Return of Light is a lyrical work of literary fiction inspired by real events. Written with emotional precision and elegant restraint, it traces a woman's journey through loss — not as spectacle, but as lived experience — and the delicate, inward ways in which hope begins to return.

About the Book

The story follows a woman whose life is reshaped by profound loss, and the subtle transformations that unfold in its aftermath. Though fictional in structure, the narrative carries the unmistakable truth of lived experience, offering a universal reflection on love, grief, and renewal.

Rather than rushing toward resolution, The Return of Light honours stillness, memory, and continuity. It explores how love does not disappear with loss, but changes form — becoming an inner architecture that quietly shapes what comes next.

This is a book for readers drawn to emotional depth, restraint, and the enduring question of how we live on without leaving ourselves behind.

"Love is the light that grief cannot extinguish."

Who This Book Is For

This book may resonate with readers who:

  • Appreciate literary fiction written with emotional restraint
  • Have experienced love and loss, and value quiet reflection
  • Are drawn to stories of inner transformation
  • Seek meaning, continuity, and hope without sentimentality

About the Author

Amy Peters has lived and worked across several countries. After decades in international trade and sustainability leadership, she now works as a leadership advisor.

The Return of Light is her debut work.

She lives and writes in Switzerland.
